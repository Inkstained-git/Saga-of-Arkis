# Nullxara Freytl
Born to the low-ranking House Freytl in Menzoberranzan, Nullxara was, at birth, nobody of note.  Seeking prestige as she grew older, she took over a minor chapel to the Spider-Goddess Lolth, patron deity of all Drow.
One morning, a Drider servant of her patron informed her that she was being sent to [[Whitelake]] as a sacrifice to [[The Nine Gods]], as part of an agreement with the Queen of the Demonweb Pits.
While devastated at being sacrificed, Nullxara obliged, despite being convinced she was being sent to the surface world to die on an altar. However, to her great surprise, the priests in the holy city informed her that she was now to be one of their followers, not a mere blood offering.
During the events leading up to the [[Transition of Magic]], Nullxara joined up with a party of adventurers, lending her aid and expertise to the cause, and along the way, she picked up a holy relic known only as [[Branch]] from a powerful and wise friend, capable of creating new trees, and also made use of a magical Wish to alter her blood and warlock pact from Lolth to cleanse the black sand that composed the [[Burn Scars]].
Devoting her life to stemming the encroachment of the [[Boundless Desert]] using these tools, Nullxara became single-handedly responsible for the [[Forest of the Spider]] along the northern edge of the dunes - a great enchanted forest of mystery and power.
Sadly, as part of the events culminating in the death of [[The First Red]] and issues surrounding the transferred [[Mantle]] of [[Abditus]] left her most mentally unwell, as the feelings of being abandoned by Lolth caught up to her; however she was ultimately saved by [[Kiyomi, Queen of Hell]] in the aftermath, and went on to complete her work with the great forest. In response to her efforts to curtail the desert sands, Inevitables known as [[Anhydrut]], created to punish those who would terraform arid regions, began to ambush her, and while she has not been seen by mortals for twenty years, the continued presence of Anhydrut, still seeming to be hunting their target, is indicative that her life is not over yet.
