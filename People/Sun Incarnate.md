# The Sun Incarnate
The Sun Incarnate, rather than a single person, is the position held by the current monarch of the Sun Elf Empire. Forsaking any name, familial relations, or prior identity, the Sun Incarnate is upheld as a living embodiment of the Sun Most Holy itself.

Currently, there is no Sun Incarnate - the most recent one having been apotheosised into [[The Living Eclipse]] and ascending to a position in [[The Nine Gods|The Nine]]. Before their apotheosis, they were the one who ruled the city of [[Solhom]] during its time shielded under solar magic in [[The Sun Elf Defence]] until its destruction due to the machinations of [[The First Red]].

The Sun Incarnate prior was the one who began the [[War of the Sun]], attempting to lead a war of conquest across [[Boundless Desert|The Blessed Dunes]] against the outside invaders, as she saw it - a war that ultimately led to her death on the point of a spear, and her blood on the walls of her beloved city.

Until the grand palace in [[Gran Solhom]] is complete, there will be no new Sun Incarnate, and until there is, [[Solar Regent Rinha]] holds power in trust for the next incarnation of the Sun Most Holy.
