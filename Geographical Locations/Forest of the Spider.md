Created by the Spider-Mage [[Nullxara]] in an attempt to curb the spread of the [[Boundless Desert]]. It runs along the northern edge of the desert, and goes into the [[Barrier Mountains]].
The forest, ever since its creation, has been filled with all manner of creatures, most prominently fey - faeries and pixies and animals that seem too intelligent when they look at would-be hunters and trappers.
