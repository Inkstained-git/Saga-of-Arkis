# Whitelake

On the eastern edge of [[White Lake]], a mighty waterfall sends the pure, fresh water cascading into the [[Bluecap Sea]] below. Built of the shining white stone that gives the lake its name, on an island above the falls, the grand city of Whitelake stands. Walled and dominated by nine soaring towers of alabaster stone, the city is the oldest upon the continent of [[Terultimae]], and the center of worship for [[The Nine]].

### Culture

The people of Whitelake are, by and large, very religious - the default assumption of any citizen is that they worship at least one of the Nine, most often more. The culture is tied to the waters of the White Lake - a yearly cycle of flood and fallow dictating the seasons and calendar first established by the city that proved the foundation for the reckoning of the year used by the rest of the continent.

### History

The city was founded long ago, by sailors from across the Bluecap Sea, fleeing some untold disaster - the specifics of which are lost to the mists of time. Landing upon the shore of Terultimae, they found a vast freshwater lake - closer to an inland sea, in some senses - and settled upon the clifftops.

Notably, the peoples of Whitelake are the ones who made [[The Bargain With The Nine]], providing mortals with a degree of protection from the whims of the divine.

### Territories

The city of Whitelake controls a nation known as [[The Nonary Land]]; reaching as far north as the [[Iceflats]] and the [[Valley of Swords]], and as far south as the beginnings of the [[Boundless Desert]]. Many small villages and towns exist within this nation, usually with one of the Nine as their individual patron; this means that the churches of those individual deities are responsible for the management and tithing of those settlements.

### Religion

As may be expected of the religious center of power on the continent, the [[Church of The Nine]] is the predominant religion in Whitelake. However, the further you get from the city itself, the less strongly this influence is felt; outlying regions often paying little more than lip service to the Nine - although there are strong believers in even the furthest settlements.

### Trade & Transport

Ever since the Teleportation Circle network started to be established, Whitelake has taken to it eagerly - wanting to reduce the logistical load that comes from transporting goods across their own nation and to their neighbours. Shipments are regularly sent to each outlying settlement, as well as a steady flow of goods to both [[Arkis's Grand Academy of the Arcane|The Academy]] and [[New Coastview|Coastview]].
