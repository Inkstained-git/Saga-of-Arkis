# Scorn
Located in the exact geographical center of the [[Boundless Desert]] due to a quirk of how [[Anhydrut]] hunt, the settlement of Scorn is notorious for its ill repute.
In Scorn, there are no peacekeepers, there are no connections to the outside world, the only authority is the town's founder and barkeep, known only as [[Jak]], and the only two rules are "Do not fight in Jak's bar" and "If you attract the Anhydrut, you will be staked to the ground outside of town and left to die".
Officially, in the records available in [[New Coastview|Coastview]], there is no such place as Scorn. The only ways to find it are to wander the trackless, featureless desert in search of it or be force-teleported there by an Anhydrut.
Jak's bar, the Scorpion's Sting, is the only consistent business in Scorn - all others are fleeting, as most people who reside there only choose to do so long enough to evade the authorities wherever they come from; nobody but Jak stays in Scorn longer than they absolutely need to.
