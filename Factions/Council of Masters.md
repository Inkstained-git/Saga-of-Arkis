Ruling [[Arkis's Grand Academy of the Arcane]], the Council of Masters is comprised of Six Masters, selected from the wisest and most accomplished [[Sage]]s in the Academy.
The current Masters are:
Mir, the [[Master of Sky]], current Headmaster of the Academy
Alai, the [[Master of Crafts]],
Arha, the [[Master of Shadows]],
Elia, the [[Master of Names]],
Iar, the [[Master of Metal]],
and Han, the [[Master of Life]]

Historically, the role of the Council of Masters is to oversee and administrate the Academy, and by extension, matters of importance to the Arcane across all of [[Terultimae]]. The original Council was the six students of [[Arkis]] herself, and ever since, their job has been to pass on the teachings of the First Mage to all students, and carry forward her wisdom long after she is gone.
