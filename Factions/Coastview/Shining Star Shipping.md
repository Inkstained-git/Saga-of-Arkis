*"Securing shipments by starlight."*

Led by [[Coordinator Skael]], Shining Star Shipping is the backbone of all freight, logistics, and transportation needs in [[The Greater Administrature of Coastview]]. They maintain a dense network of warehouses, Teleportation Circle hubs, and golem-based overland shipping, as well as a token amount of coastal ships, equipped to stick close to the shore and support trade with [[Whitelake]] and [[Dalhal]]. Any citizen of the Administrature may hire SSS for whatever their transit needs - for a modest fee.
Political policies that they financially back are generally those centered and based around increasing commerce between [[New Coastview]] and its neighbours, investing in improving the infrastructure required to maintain long-term trade relationships.

Behind the pleasant front of providing a needed public service, however, Shining Star Shipping is in actuality a front for a circle of thieves, burglers, and fences - specialising in the retrieval of one-of-a-kind items from private collections. Anybody in the know can hire a "retrieval specialist" to obtain nearly anything they need, for the right price.
