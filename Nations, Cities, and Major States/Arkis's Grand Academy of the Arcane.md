Situated on the eastern coast of [[Terultimae]] and south of [[Whitelake]], the Academy floats high above the [[Boundless Desert]], with the [[Bluecap Sea]] to the east. To the south-west, [[New Coastview]] stands, a new ally since their rebuilding.
Floating on the great earthmotes, the Academy was founded a great many years ago by [[Arkis]], known as "The First Mage".
Run now by the [[Council of Masters]], the Academy claims jurisdiction over all arcane magic everywhere, including all mythals and magical phenomena - including the [[Laws of Magic]], a triad of rules that are supposed to keep order and uphold the legacy of Arkis across the known world.
Education is divided into [[Mage]] and [[Sage]] accreditations, and all Mages and Sages are awarded an [[Icon]], which serves as a form of identification and license for performing magic.
Those who break the laws of the Academy are known as [[Apostates]], and the enforcement branch of the Academy is known as the [[Seekers]] - a corps of specialist Mages whose job it is to track down errant magical phenomena, and amongst other things, bringing Apostates in for punishment.
Criminality in the eyes of the Academy is punishable by a ritual known as [[Unnaming]], in which magic is used to fundamentally alter aspects of the person's very being, in order to remove their magical ability, 'so they cannot err with it in future'. The people so altered are known as the [[Unnamed]], and they are generally let free after the ritual's completion - although common wisdom holds that anybody so sundered loses the will to live before long, and reports of suicide amongst the Unnamed are common.
Politically speaking, the Academy tries to present itself as a body that is separate and above matters of nations, but it is in effect a nation in and of itself - to the point of having its own political factions, known as the [[Traditionalists]], who argue that the wisdom of prior laws is sacrosanct and the way to improve upon them is by adding additional laws, and the [[Progressives]], who argue that wisdom should be refined over time and that existing laws should be revised as their reasons for existing fall in and out of applicability.
Presently, on the Council, two Masters are Progressives (the [[Master of Sky]] and the [[Master of Shadows]]), three are Traditionalist (the [[Master of Metal]], the [[Master of Crafts]], and the [[Master of Life]]), and one is a fence-sitter who tries not to let herself be swayed to either  party (the [[Master of Names]])
