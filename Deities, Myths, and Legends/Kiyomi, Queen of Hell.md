### The Hell-Queen, The Fox-Goddess, She of Swirling Tails

Until recently a mortal, Kiyomi's apotheosis, while it happened at the same time as that of [[The Living Eclipse]], is far less known and understood. What is known is that her Mantle was formerly owned by Asmodeus, and that the Nine aided in the transfer of power as simultaneous punishment of Asmodeus and a reward for Kiyomi - although for what, no mortal can rightly say.

Usually manifesting as a curvaceous and colossal kitsune, lavishly dressed and deliberately eye-catching, the Fox-Goddess spends the majority of her time running the Nine Hells; having devoted herself entirely to her new Mantle, maintaining order in the underworld and continuing the Blood War, and perpetuating diabolic pacts for those mortals who wish them.

### Divine Domains

Pacts, Devils, Stone, Fire, Death, Enchantment, Illusion

### Artifacts

Rumour tells of an odd phenomenon that occurs in rare circumstances; if one of the avatars of Kiyomi is slain, it crumbles into red-and-white stone that is fatal to the touch to any mortal - this fox-stone exists in very limited quantities and is jealously hoarded by the [[Cult of the Swirled Tails]].

### Divine Symbols & Sigils

Nine curved lines, curling out from a central point.
