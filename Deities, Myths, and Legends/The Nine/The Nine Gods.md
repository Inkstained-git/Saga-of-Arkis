The Nine Gods are the circle of deities that are the primary targets of worship in settlements across [[Terultimae]].

The center of their church is in [[Whitelake]], and the [[Church of the Nine Gods]] is the largest single organization not affiliated with [[Arkis's Grand Academy of the Arcane]].

The current members of the Nine are:

[[Kyna]], Matron of Hope and Despair

[[Scias]], Scholar of Curiosity and Ignorance

[[The Living Eclipse]], The Hidden and The Revealed

[[Inere]], Goddess of Joy and Sorrow

[[Tenemore]], Lady of Victory and Loss

[[Krestenzia]], Mistress of Creation and Destruction

[[Vlexem]], She of Naivete and Cynicism

[[Davaris]], Lord of Sound and Silence

[[Xyco]], Master of Stillness and Movement

Until the [[Transition of Magic]], [[Abditus]], Lord of Truth and Lies, was a member of the Nine, before [[The First Red]] attempted to steal their divinity. Their position was subsequently inherited by The Living Eclipse .

Long ago, the peoples of Whitelake struck [[The Bargain With The Nine]], and as part of this bargain, the Nine created a great magical working known as the [[Divine Aegis]]. This spell has lasted through the ages since, protecting mortals broadly from the evils that less benevolent deities may inflict.
