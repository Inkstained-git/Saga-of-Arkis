### Lady of Victory and Loss, The Battle Queen

Tenemore, The Battle Queen, is known as the patron of soldiers, warriors, and combatants everywhere - as well as the patron of diplomats, negotiators, and peacebrokers.

Usually taking the form of of a tall, dark-haired orc female, The Lady of Victory and Loss clads herself in golden armour, preaching of the importance of knowing when to make peace - and when to fight for what needs fighting for.

### Divine Domains

Luck, Evening, Void, Violence, War, Negotiating

### Artifacts

The most holy relic of Tenemore is a round shield, gilded and chipped - said by her priesthood to offer complete and utter protection as long as it is used to shield a non-combatant at your side. The Golden Shield has been missing for decades, but it has a recurring pattern of appearing on the most unlikely of battlefields, as if blessing some escortee with the Lady's personal protection.

### Divine Symbols & Sigils

A stylised skull as the end of a flail

### Holidays

Signalled by the falling of the first leaves of autumn, a grand contest and festival is held; the Shining Tourney. Anybody who wishes may submit themselves to the Church of Tenemore to sign up, and a series of wargames, mock-battles, duels, and contests of prowess are held over the next nine days, culminating in a victor known as The Lady's Champion - a title they may hold for the next year in their community - ending in a two-day party in which the Champion hosts all of their vanquished foes at their home in a demonstration of sportsmanship and peacemaking.
