# Abditus, Lord of Truth and Lies, It that Hides, Secret-Keeper

Manifestations of the Secret-Keeper were essentially unheard-of, even when It still was a living God. It was responsible for managing the secrets of Whitelake, all things hidden in the dark, and managing magic - and when the [[Transition of Magic]] came, larger disaster was narrowly averted because Abditus was willing to sacrifice Its own life and divinity, causing the apotheosis and ascension of [[The Living Eclipse]], as well as foiling a centuries-long plan of the [[The First Red]] that would have spelled disaster for all mortals if it had succeeded.

### Divine Domains

Magic, Midnight, Darkness, Intelligence, Secrets, Shadows

### Artifacts

It is said that, in the days before [[The Bargain With The Nine]], the Lord of Lies would wear a powerful magical artefact - a mythal in the shape of a mask - that permitted It to wander amongst mortals without them remembering It. Rumours and murmurations about what has become of this mythal persist across [[Terultimae]] - but if it indeed exists at all, there have been no confirmed sightings in many years.

### Divine Symbols & Sigils
