# Inere, Goddess of Joy and Sorrow, Fairy Queen of Emotions, Lady of Feeling

The Church of Inere tells that usually, the Lady of Feeling will manifest in the form of a blonde humanoid woman; slightly above-average in height and with long hair and fairy-like wings, akin to those of a dragonfly.  
She is the patron of performers, as well as emotion in all its forms - positive and negative. Amongst [[The Nine Gods]], she is the one who represents the Seelie and Unseelie of the Feywilds, as well as interacts most often with the Gods of Fury - maintaining the [[Divine Aegis]] against their whims most directly.  

### Divine Domains

Emotion, Afternoon, Lightning, Anger, Madness, Mourning

### Artifacts

Held by the Inerian Church is a sleeveless tunic, made of a brightly-coloured fabric more fine than any mortal hand can create; it is said that this tunic was once worn by the Lady of Feeling on the day she apotheosised. It is unclear the veracity of this legend, but the tunic is held in a secure place by the Church, treated as a holy relic.

### Divine Symbols & Sigils

Fairy wings, composed of four ovals attached at a central point

### Holidays

On the first day of summer, it is a decree of the Fairy Queen of Emotion that all her followers should gather and celebrate; an event known as Essenia. Relaxing, sharing platters of food (traditionally rice, poultry, seafood), watching plays and similar performances - whatever things bring you the most joy and positive emotions.  
On the evening, the Church suggests finding a quiet place with someone you trust and love, and sharing your other emotions - your sorrows, your angers, the things you grieve and the things you mourn. Once these things are shared, you should put them from your mind, and on the morrow, greet the summer afresh, letting joy lift your heart.
