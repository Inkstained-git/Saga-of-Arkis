# The Transition of Magic
As part of the events surrounding [[Abditus]]'s Divine [[Mantle]] being passed to [[The Living Eclipse]], there was a six-second span of time in which all magical effects failed. Golems fell idle, magical creatures slipped into animal instinct, mythals failed - and when magic restarted again, six seconds later, which effects resumed function seemed to be determined essentially at random.  
Mages from [[Arkis's Grand Academy of the Arcane|The Academy]] attempted to repair the damage, but the sheer insurmountability of trying to catalogue all of the magical effects that failed has been a continuous load on their resources ever since.
